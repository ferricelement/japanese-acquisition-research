# Routes and keys

## core (route "core") — content words learned as words
- v-move: Moving & position (walk, run, sit, stand, approach, pass, return home, move house)
- v-body: Body actions, senses & physical states (laugh, cry, sleep, fall over, hit, touch, smell, shake)
- v-home: Daily routine, home & chores (wash, cook, clean, tidy, dress, prepare, live somewhere)
- v-talk: Talking & communicating (call, tell, explain, apologise, refuse, chat, answer, invite, ask a favour)
- v-mind: Thinking, knowing & feeling (believe, feel, remember, notice, worry, be surprised, be glad, doubt, understand)
- v-social: People & relationships (help, forgive, trust, protect, leave it to, betray, go out with, marry, break up)
- v-change: Change, start & end, more & less (continue, increase, disappear, break, change, get used to, be decided)
- v-hands: Handling things (put, hand over, hide, find, collect, pick up, carry, deliver, receive, lose, fix)
- v-effort: Work, effort & results (succeed, fail, win, lose, choose, be in time, check, practise, aim)
- v-trouble: Trouble, conflict & danger (fight, steal, hurt, deceive, kill, run away, attack, get caught, threaten)
- v-life: Life & living (be born, live, die, grow up, spend time, raise)
- n-people: People, roles & relationships (person, woman, man, friend, senior, boss, customer, partner, couple)
- n-life: Life, time & fate (life, future, past, memory, era, moment, chance, dream)
- n-mind: Mind, feelings & character (feeling, happiness, anxiety, courage, confidence, personality, love)
- n-abstract: Reasons, situations & ideas (reason, relation, case, situation, way, result, problem, meaning, fact, cause, purpose)
- n-talk: Talk & information (story, opinion, reply, news, rumour, secret, lie, promise, question, answer)
- n-places: Places & buildings (place, room, shop, station, hospital, school, company, town, country, world)
- n-home: Home & everyday things (furniture, keys, wallet, phone, photo, letter, present, bag, clothes)
- n-food: Food & drink (meal, rice, water, tea, alcohol, meat, fish, vegetables, sweets)
- n-work: Work & school (job, meeting, documents, exam, class, homework, salary, part-time job)
- n-health: Body, health & medicine (illness, injury, medicine, fever, cold, hospital visit, wound)
- n-society: Society, rules & news (police, incident, accident, law, news, politics, rules, crime)
- n-travel: Getting around & going out (train, car, trip, road, airport, hotel, date, event, party)
- n-money: Money & shopping (money, price, payment, card, receipt, discount, cheap shop)
- n-nature: Nature, animals & the outdoors (sky, sea, mountain, flower, tree, dog, cat, river)
- n-fun: Free time, media & tech (hobby, film, music, game, match, song, smartphone, internet)
- n-things: General nouns: things, kinds, parts & amounts (thing, shape, sound, voice, name, number, kind, half, rest)
- a-feel: Adjectives of feeling & sensation (sad, lonely, frustrating, nostalgic, painful, sleepy, itchy, scary-feeling)
- a-people: Adjectives describing people (strict, serious, honest, clever, young, admirable, cool, mean)
- a-things: Adjectives describing things & situations (old, bright, dark, heavy, wide, far, strong, strange, special, safe, free)
- adv-manner: Adverbs of manner, certainty & timing (definitely, properly, on purpose, suddenly, finally, directly, at once)
- adv-degree: Degree words the main list lacks (totemo, zuibun, wari to, mattaku, daibu, sara ni, masumasu)
- adv-mimetic: Sound & feeling words / mimetics (dokidoki, wakuwaku, iraira, gussuri, pittari, girigiri, perapera)
- connect: Connectors & discourse words the main list lacks (soshite, sore ni, tokoro de, sore demo, sore to mo, chinami ni)

## set (route "set") — closed sets learned whole
- s-numbers: Sino-Japanese numbers 0-10, 100, 1,000, 10,000, 100 million, with reading variants and sound changes (yon/shi, nana/shichi, sanbyaku, roppyaku, sanzen, hassen)
- s-native: Native counting: hitotsu ... tou, ikutsu
- s-people: Counting people: hitori, futari, sannin ... nannin
- s-counters: Everyday counters and their sound changes: -ko, -hon, -mai, -hiki, -satsu, -dai, -hai, -kai (times), -kai (floor), -ban, -banme, -do, -en, -bai
- s-clock: Clock time & durations: -ji (yoji, shichiji, kuji), -fun/-pun, han, nanji, -jikan, -funkan, -byou
- s-weekdays: Days of the week, nan'youbi, heijitsu
- s-dates: Days of the month: tsuitachi, futsuka ... touka, juuyokka, hatsuka, nijuuyokka, nannichi, -nichikan
- s-months: Months & month counts: ichigatsu ... juunigatsu, nangatsu, ikkagetsu, nankagetsu
- s-years: Years & age: -nen, -nenkan, -sai (issai, hassai, jussai, hatachi), nansai / oikutsu, -nensei
- s-relative: Relative-time grid words the main list lacks: ototoi, asatte, sensenshuu, saraishuu, ototoshi, sarainen, maiasa, maiban, maishuu, maitsuki, mainen
- s-dayparts: Parts of the day & meals: yuugata, yonaka, gozen, gogo, asagohan, hirugohan, bangohan (asa/hiru/yoru are in the main list)
- s-seasons: Seasons & weather: haru, natsu, aki, fuyu, hare, kumori, yuki, kaze, taifuu, atatakai, suzushii, mushiatsui
- s-colors: Colours: aka/akai, ao/aoi, kiiro, shiro/shiroi, kuro/kuroi, chairo, midori, murasaki, pinku, orenji, haiiro, iro
- s-family: Family words, own vs someone else's (chichi/otousan, haha/okaasan, ani/oniisan ...) and the casual home forms (papa, mama, oniichan)
- s-body: Body parts: atama, kao, me, mimi, hana, kuchi, ha, kubi, kata, te, ude, yubi, mune, onaka, senaka, koshi, ashi, hiza, kami
- s-position: Position & direction the main list lacks: migi, hidari, tonari, yoko, soba, aida, mukou, oku, massugu, kado, kita, minami, higashi, nishi
- s-pronouns: Personal pronouns & people-pointers the main list lacks: ore, atashi, anata, kimi, omae, anta, kare, kanojo, aitsu, koitsu, soitsu, yatsu, -ra, wareware
- s-countries: Countries, languages & nationalities: nihon, -go, -jin, amerika, igirisu, chuugoku, kankoku, gaikoku
- s-taste: Tastes & textures: amai, karai, shoppai, suppai, nigai, mazui, katai, yawarakai

## phrase (route "phrase") — formulas said as a whole
- ph-greet: Greetings, partings & home rituals (konbanwa, sayounara, mata ne, ittekimasu, tadaima, okaeri)
- ph-thanks: Thanks & apologies (arigatou gozaimasu, doumo, sumimasen deshita, moushiwake arimasen, gomen nasai)
- ph-manners: Polite formulas (shitsurei shimasu, ojama shimasu, osewa ni natte imasu, yoroshiku onegai shimasu, osaki ni)
- ph-ask: Requests, offers & permission (onegai dekimasu ka, ii desu ka, tetsudaou ka, dou shitara ii?)
- ph-shop: Shopping & paying (kore kudasai, ikura desu ka, kaado de ii desu ka, fukuro wa daijoubu desu)
- ph-eat: Eating & drinking (kanpai, oishisou, onaka suita, okawari, okaikei onegai shimasu)
- ph-staff: What staff say to you (irasshaimase, shoushou omachi kudasai, omatase shimashita, kashikomarimashita)
- ph-phone: Phone, messages & meeting up (ima daijoubu?, ato de kakenaosu, tsuita yo, chotto okureru)
- ph-care: Care, sympathy & encouragement (odaiji ni, muri shinaide, taihen datta ne, ki ni shinaide)
- ph-celebrate: Congratulations & seasonal wishes (otanjoubi omedetou, akemashite omedetou, yoi otoshi o)
- ph-react: Reactions & exclamations (yatta, yoshi, uwa, maji de?, shimatta, mou!)
- ph-talk: Keeping talk going & clarifying (ano sa, chotto kiite, dou omou?, sou iu koto ka)
- ph-trouble: Problems & emergencies (tasukete, michi ni mayotta, saifu o nakushita, kyuukyuusha o yonde)
- ph-feel: Saying how you are (tsukareta, nemui, onaka ippai, tanoshikatta, kinchou suru)

## pattern (route "pattern", key "pattern") — grammar expressions that attach to words or clauses (koto ni naru, kamoshirenai, wake ni wa ikanai, ni totte, no sei de)
