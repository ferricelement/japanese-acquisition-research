# Stage 1 listening stories

Short listening stories built from Stage 1 of the versatility list. Stories 1–9 go from easiest to hardest; stories 10–13 are practice stories built around the Stage 1 words the first nine use least (greetings, degree words, polite past forms, sequencing). For each one: listen without looking at any text, answer the five questions, then open Answers and transcript to check. Each line gives the Japanese for your voice tool, with the speaker and a voice hint; if the voice misreads a kanji, use the kana version of that line instead. Words outside Stage 1 are listed under New words.

## 1. Bumping into a friend

*Casual dialogue · 11 lines · Kenta, Yuki*

Old friends Yuki and Kenta run into each other on a Tokyo street: he is on his way to work, and she is on her way to a shop.

### Audio script

| Speaker | Voice |
|---|---|
| Kenta | male, late 20s, relaxed and friendly |
| Yuki | female, late 20s, bright and friendly |

| # | Speaker | Japanese | Kana (if the voice misreads a kanji) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Kenta | ユキ！久しぶり！ | ゆき！ひさしぶり！ |
| 2 | Yuki | ケンタ！久しぶりだね！今からどこ行くの？ | けんた！ひさしぶりだね！いまからどこいくの？ |
| 3 | Kenta | 会社。ユキは？うちに帰るの？ | かいしゃ。ゆきは？うちにかえるの？ |
| 4 | Yuki | ううん、あそこの店。 | ううん、あそこのみせ。 |
| 5 | Kenta | なんで？ | なんで？ |
| 6 | Yuki | うちにお茶がもうないんだ。 | うちにおちゃがもうないんだ。 |
| 7 | Kenta | でも、あの店、今日は休みだよ。今あそこから来たんだ。 | でも、あのみせ、きょうはやすみだよ。いまあそこからきたんだ。 |
| 8 | Yuki | ほんとに？じゃあ、今日はうちに帰る。 | ほんとに？じゃあ、きょうはうちにかえる。 |
| 9 | Kenta | うん。明日はやってるよ。 | うん。あしたはやってるよ。 |
| 10 | Yuki | 明日行くね。ありがとう、じゃあね！ | あしたいくね。ありがとう、じゃあね！ |
| 11 | Kenta | じゃあね！ | じゃあね！ |

### Questions

1. **What happens in this conversation?**  
   nani ga arimashita ka? (何がありましたか？)
   - A. Yuki and Kenta go to the shop together today.
   - B. The shop is closed today, so Yuki will go tomorrow.
   - C. Kenta's office is closed today, so he goes home.

2. **Where is Kenta going?**  
   kenta-san wa, doko ni ikimasu ka? (ケンタさんは、どこに行きますか？)
   - A. To his office
   - B. Home
   - C. To the shop

3. **Why was Yuki going to the shop?**  
   yuki-san wa, nande sono mise ni iku n desu ka? (ユキさんは、なんでその店に行くんですか？)
   - A. The shop is only open today.
   - B. She has the day off today.
   - C. She's out of tea at home.

4. **Kenta says, "demo, ano mise, kyou wa yasumi da yo." What does he mean by ending with "yo"?**  
   "kyou wa yasumi da yo." koko no "yo" wa, nan desu ka? (「今日は休みだよ。」ここの「よ」は、何ですか？)
   - A. He's checking something they both already know.
   - B. He's telling Yuki something she doesn't know yet.
   - C. He's wondering out loud whether the shop is open.

5. **Yuki asks Kenta, "ima kara doko iku no?" Imagine she asks you. What could you say back?**  
   ima kara doko iku no? (今からどこ行くの？)

### New words

- **mise** (店): shop, store
- **kaisha** (会社): company; the office (going to work)
- **ocha** (お茶): tea (green tea)
- **yasumi** (休み): closed; a day off
- **yatteru** (やってる): (of a shop) is open, doing business; from yaru

<details>
<summary>Answers and transcript</summary>

1. **B**. Line 4: Yuki is heading to the shop over there. Line 7: Kenta tells her it's closed today (kyou wa yasumi). Line 8: she decides to go home today (kyou wa uchi ni kaeru). Line 10: she'll go tomorrow (ashita iku ne). A is wrong because Kenta is going to work (line 3) and they say goodbye (lines 10-11). C is wrong because the shop is the thing that's closed (yasumi, line 7), and Kenta is on his way to his company (line 3).
2. **A**. Line 3: Kenta answers 'kaisha', his company, so he's going to work. 'uchi ni kaeru no?' in the same line is his guess about Yuki, not his own plan. The shop is where Yuki was going (line 4). Kenta has just come from there (line 7: ima asoko kara kita n da), and he isn't going back.
3. **C**. Line 5: Kenta asks 'nande?'. Line 6: Yuki answers 'uchi ni ocha ga mou nai n da': there's no tea left at home, and n da gives it as her reason. A is the opposite of what happens: the shop is closed today and open tomorrow (lines 7 and 9). B misplaces yasumi: in line 7 the shop is the thing that's closed (yasumi), and no one says Yuki has a day off.
4. **B**. Line 7: yo marks news for the listener. Yuki didn't know: she was on her way there (lines 4-6) and reacts with 'honto ni?' (line 8). Kenta is sure because he just came from there (ima asoko kara kita n da). To check something they both know, he would say 'yo ne' (A). To wonder out loud, he would say 'kana' (C).
5. Say where you're going now (home, over there). Maybe give a reason with n da, or ask her back with '... wa?' For example: *uchi ni kaeru yo. yuki wa?* (I'm going home. How about you, Yuki?); *asoko ni iku n da. yuki mo kuru?* (I'm heading over there. Want to come too, Yuki?). Yuki asks Kenta this in line 2. His answer in line 3 ('kaisha. yuki wa?') shows the pattern: name the place, then ask back with '... wa?'. The Japanese prompt is Yuki's casual line itself, not a polite question, because you are answering her as a friend.

| 3 | Kenta | kaisha. yuki wa? uchi ni kaeru no? | Work. How about you, Yuki? Going home? |

## 2. Sorry I'm late

*Casual dialogue · 10 lines · Mai, Sora*

Mai rushes up late to meet her friend Sora in town, apologises, and hands him a coffee she brought.

### Audio script

| Speaker | Voice |
|---|---|
| Mai | female, 20s, bright, a little out of breath |
| Sora | male, 20s, relaxed, gently teasing |

| # | Speaker | Japanese | Kana (if the voice misreads a kanji) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Mai | ソラ！まだいた！ごめん、ちょっと遅くなっちゃった！ | そら！まだいた！ごめん、ちょっとおそくなっちゃった！ |
| 2 | Sora | いいよ、いいよ。大丈夫。 | いいよ、いいよ。だいじょうぶ。 |
| 3 | Mai | だって、電車がずっと来なかったから。 | だって、でんしゃがずっとこなかったから。 |
| 4 | Sora | 電車か。だから遅くなったんだ。じゃあ、マイは悪くないよ。 | でんしゃか。だからおそくなったんだ。じゃあ、まいはわるくないよ。 |
| 5 | Mai | ソラは、いつ来たの？ | そらは、いつきたの？ |
| 6 | Sora | もうずっと待ってたよ。ちょっとじゃないよね。 | もうずっとまってたよ。ちょっとじゃないよね。 |
| 7 | Mai | ほんとにごめんね。これ、どうぞ。 | ほんとにごめんね。これ、どうぞ。 |
| 8 | Sora | え、それ何？ | え、それなに？ |
| 9 | Mai | コーヒー。ソラに持ってきたんだ。 | コーヒー。そらにもってきたんだ。 |
| 10 | Sora | いいの？ありがとう！じゃあ、行く？ | いいの？ありがとう！じゃあ、いく？ |

### Questions

1. **What happens in this story?**  
   nani ga arimashita ka? (何がありましたか？)
   - A. Sora is late, and Mai is annoyed that she had to wait.
   - B. Mai is late, says sorry, and gives Sora something she brought.
   - C. Mai and Sora wait together for a train that never comes.

2. **What did Mai bring for Sora?**  
   mai-san wa, sora-san ni nani o motte kimashita ka? (マイさんは、ソラさんに何を持ってきましたか？)
   - A. A train ticket she didn't use.
   - B. A coffee she bought for herself.
   - C. A coffee that she brought for him.

3. **When did Sora get there?**  
   sora-san wa, itsu kimashita ka? (ソラさんは、いつ来ましたか？)
   - A. Long before Mai. He'd been waiting the whole time.
   - B. Only a little before Mai, so he didn't wait long.
   - C. He was late too, because his train didn't come.

4. **Mai says "datte, densha ga zutto konakatta kara." What is she doing with "datte"?**  
   mai-san no 'datte, densha ga zutto konakatta kara'. kono 'datte' de, mai-san wa nani o shitemasu ka? (マイさんの「だって、電車がずっと来なかったから」。この「だって」で、マイさんは何をしてますか？)
   - A. Drawing a conclusion: the train didn't come, so let's go now.
   - B. Making an excuse: it's not her fault, the train didn't come.
   - C. Asking a question: did Sora's train not come either?

5. **Sora teases Mai: "mou zutto matteta yo. chotto ja nai yo ne." If you were Mai, what could you say back?**  
   'mou zutto matteta yo. chotto ja nai yo ne' tte, sora-san ga. mai-san wa? (「もうずっと待ってたよ。ちょっとじゃないよね」って、ソラさんが。マイさんは？)

### New words

- **osoi** (遅い): late, slow (osoku natchatta = I ended up late; osoku natta = got late)
- **densha** (電車): train
- **matsu** (待つ): wait (matteta = was waiting)
- **e** (え): huh? (surprise, rising tone; not the particle e)
- **koohii** (コーヒー): coffee

<details>
<summary>Answers and transcript</summary>

1. **B**. Line 1: Mai says gomen, chotto osoku natchatta (sorry, I'm a bit late). Lines 7-9: she hands Sora something (kore, douzo), and it turns out to be a coffee. A swaps the two people: Sora is the one who waited (line 6). C mixes up the train: only Mai's train wouldn't come (line 3), and they never wait for one together.
2. **C**. Line 9: koohii. sora ni motte kita n da (Coffee. I brought it for you). Sora ni means it's for him, and in line 10 he asks ii no? (For me?) and thanks her. B is wrong because the coffee is for Sora, not for her. A is wrong because the train only comes up as her excuse (line 3), and she never gives him a ticket.
3. **A**. Line 5: Mai asks sora wa, itsu kita no? (when did you get here?). Line 6: Sora doesn't give a time. He says mou zutto matteta yo (I've been waiting the whole time), then chotto ja nai yo ne (that's not 'a bit', is it?), turning Mai's own chotto from line 1 back on her. Line 1 (mada ita! = you're still here!) also shows he was there first. B repeats that chotto, which Sora says isn't true. C mixes up the train: only Mai's train wouldn't come (line 3).
4. **B**. Line 3: datte opens an excuse ('it's just that...'), and kara at the end gives the reason: the train wouldn't come. Sora accepts it in line 4: jaa, mai wa warukunai yo (then it's not your fault). A describes dakara ('so'), which draws a conclusion. Sora uses it that way in line 4 (dakara osoku natta n da = so that's why you were late), and it's Sora, not Mai, who suggests going (line 10). C is wrong because the line ends in kara without a rising question tone, and nobody mentions Sora's train.
5. Apologise again, warmly, e.g. 'honto ni gomen ne.' or 'gomen. watashi ga warukatta.' For example: *honto ni gomen ne.* (I'm really sorry.); *gomen. watashi ga warukatta.* (Sorry. That was my fault.). Line 6 is a friendly tease, not real anger: he already said ii yo, daijoubu in line 2 and mai wa warukunai yo in line 4. The natural comeback is another apology, the way Mai does in line 7 (honto ni gomen ne). warukatta is the past of warui: 'I was the one at fault.'

|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Mai | sora! mada ita! gomen, chotto osoku natchatta! | Sora! You're still here! Sorry, I'm a bit late! |
| 2 | Sora | ii yo, ii yo. daijoubu. | It's fine, it's fine. No problem. |
| 3 | Mai | datte, densha ga zutto konakatta kara. | It's just, the train wouldn't come. |
| 4 | Sora | densha ka. dakara osoku natta n da. jaa, mai wa warukunai yo. | The train, huh. So that's why you were late. Then it's not your fault. |

| 7 | Mai | honto ni gomen ne. kore, douzo. | I'm really sorry. Here, this is for you. |
| 8 | Sora | e, sore nani? | Huh? What's that? |

| 10 | Sora | ii no? arigatou! jaa, iku? | For me? Thanks! Okay, shall we go? |

## 3. Whose is this?

*Casual dialogue · 11 lines · Kenta, Sora*

At home, Kenta finds an umbrella, a key and a bag lying around and asks his flatmate Sora whose they are.

### Audio script

| Speaker | Voice |
|---|---|
| Kenta | male, 20s, relaxed, easygoing |
| Sora | female, 20s, cheerful, clear |

| # | Speaker | Japanese | Kana (if the voice misreads a kanji) |

| 3 | Kenta | あそこにあったよ。じゃあ、この鍵は？これもソラの？ | あそこにあったよ。じゃあ、このかぎは？これもそらの？ |
| 4 | Sora | ううん、私のじゃないよ。自分の鍵、持ってるし。 | ううん、わたしのじゃないよ。じぶんのかぎ、もってるし。 |
| 5 | Kenta | 僕のもここにあるし。誰のかな。 | ぼくのもここにあるし。だれのかな。 |
| 6 | Sora | その鍵、マイのだよ。昨日、マイたち、うちに来たよね。 | そのかぎ、まいのだよ。きのう、まいたち、うちにきたよね。 |
| 7 | Kenta | うん、来たね。あと、あれは？あのかばん。 | うん、きたね。あと、あれは？あのかばん。 |
| 8 | Sora | どれ？あそこの？あれは私のじゃないよ。ケンタの？ | どれ？あそこの？あれはわたしのじゃないよ。けんたの？ |
| 9 | Kenta | ううん、僕のじゃない。マイさんたち、あのかばん持ってたっけ？ | ううん、ぼくのじゃない。まいさんたち、あのかばんもってたっけ？ |
| 10 | Sora | いや、持ってなかったなあ。でも、明日みんなに聞いとくね。 | いや、もってなかったなあ。でも、あしたみんなにきいとくね。 |
| 11 | Kenta | うん、お願い。この鍵は僕が持っとくよ。 | うん、おねがい。このかぎはぼくがもっとくよ。 |

### Questions

1. **What are Kenta and Sora doing in this conversation?**  
   kenta-san to sora-san wa, nani o shitemasu ka? (ケンタさんとソラさんは、何をしてますか？)
   - A. Sora has lost her key and asks Kenta to help find it.
   - B. Kenta and Sora are planning to have Mai and her friends over tomorrow.
   - C. Kenta finds things at home, and they try to work out whose they are.

2. **Whose is the key Kenta found?**  
   uchi ni atta kagi wa, dare no desu ka? (うちにあった鍵は、誰のですか？)
   - A. Mai's
   - B. Sora's
   - C. Kenta's

3. **What will Sora do tomorrow?**  
   sora-san wa, ashita nani o shimasu ka? (ソラさんは、明日何をしますか？)
   - A. Keep the key for Mai
   - B. Have Mai and the others over again
   - C. Ask everyone about the bag

4. **Near the end, Kenta asks 'mai-san-tachi, ano kaban motteta kke?' What does the 'kke' at the end show?**  
   motteta kke tte, nan desu ka? (持ってたっけって、何ですか？)
   - A. He is sure they had the bag and is telling Sora.
   - B. He is trying to remember, and wants Sora to help him recall.
   - C. He thinks they had the bag and wants Sora to agree.

5. **Imagine Kenta holds up the umbrella and asks you 'kono kasa, dare no?', but it isn't yours. What could you say back?**  
   kono kasa, dare no? (この傘、誰の？)

### New words

- **kasa** (傘): umbrella
- **kagi** (鍵): key
- **kaban** (かばん): bag
- **kiku** (聞く): to ask (heard as kiitoku, 'I'll ask')

<details>
<summary>Answers and transcript</summary>

1. **C**. Kenta keeps asking whose things are: the umbrella (line 1), the key (line 3) and the bag (line 7). The umbrella is Sora's (line 2), the key is Mai's (line 6), and nobody knows about the bag (lines 8-10). A is wrong because Sora says she has her own key (line 4). B is wrong because Mai's group came yesterday (line 6). Tomorrow is only when Sora will ask everyone about the bag (line 10).
2. **A**. In line 4 Sora says it isn't hers because she has her own key ('jibun no kagi, motteru shi'). In line 5 Kenta says his own key is right here, so it isn't his either. In line 6 Sora says 'sono kagi, mai no da yo' (that key is Mai's).
3. **C**. In line 10 Sora says 'demo, ashita minna ni kiitoku ne' (but I'll ask everyone tomorrow). She means the bag that nobody can place (lines 8-9). Keeping the key is what Kenta says he'll do (line 11). Mai's group already came over yesterday (line 6), and nobody plans another visit.
4. **B**. 'kke' marks something you are trying to remember ('did they ... again?'). In line 9 Kenta can't recall whether Mai's group had that bag, so he checks with Sora. In line 10 she says 'iya, mottenakatta naa' (nah, I don't think they had it). He isn't telling her (that would be 'motteta yo'). He also doesn't believe they had it and want her to agree (that would be 'motteta yo ne?').
5. Say it isn't yours (watashi no / boku no ja nai) and wonder whose it is (dare no kana) or guess someone else's (sora no ja nai no?). For example: *uun, watashi no ja nai yo. dare no kana.* (No, it's not mine. I wonder whose it is.); *boku no ja nai yo. sora no ja nai no?* (It's not mine. Isn't it Sora's?). The story uses this pattern several times. Line 4 has 'watashi no ja nai yo', line 8 'are wa watashi no ja nai yo', line 9 'boku no ja nai', and line 5 'dare no kana'. Put them together to say it isn't yours and wonder or guess whose it is.

## 4. A voicemail

*Casual monologue · 8 lines · Kenta*

On a rainy evening, Kenta leaves a phone message for his friend Yuki to change their plan for tomorrow.

### Audio script

| Speaker | Voice |
|---|---|
| Kenta | male, late 20s, warm and friendly, a little tired after work, casual |

| # | Speaker | Japanese | Kana (if the voice misreads a kanji) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Kenta | もしもし、ユキ？健太だけど。 | もしもし、ゆき？けんただけど。 |
| 2 | Kenta | 明日なんだけど、ごめん、ダメになっちゃった。 | あしたなんだけど、ごめん、だめになっちゃった。 |
| 3 | Kenta | 明日も仕事が入っちゃって。今もまだ仕事してるんだ。 | あしたもしごとがはいっちゃって。いまもまだしごとしてるんだ。 |
| 4 | Kenta | でも、明日の夜は大丈夫。うちに来ない？ | でも、あしたのよるはだいじょうぶ。うちにこない？ |
| 5 | Kenta | ご飯、作っとくから。 | ごはん、つくっとくから。 |
| 6 | Kenta | 今日、ちょっと電話できる？ | きょう、ちょっとでんわできる？ |
| 7 | Kenta | 雨だから、気をつけてね。 | あめだから、きをつけてね。 |

### Questions

1. **Overall, what changed about tomorrow?**  
   ashita wa, nani ga kawarimashita ka? (明日は、何が変わりましたか。)
   - A. The plan is off, and Kenta suggests no other time.
   - B. The plan is off, but Kenta invites Yuki over tomorrow evening.
   - C. The plan moves to tonight, and Yuki cooks dinner.

2. **Why did tomorrow's plan fall through?**  
   doushite ashita wa dame ni natta n desu ka? (どうして明日はダメになったんですか。)
   - A. Because it's going to rain.
   - B. Because Yuki has to work.
   - C. Because work came up for Kenta.

3. **What does Kenta want Yuki to do today?**  
   kenta-san wa kyou, yuki-san ni nani o onegai shimashita ka? (健太さんは今日、ユキさんに何をお願いしましたか。)
   - A. Call him back today.
   - B. Come over to his place tonight.
   - C. Make dinner for him.

4. **After saying hello, Kenta says 'ashita na n da kedo...'. What is he doing with this phrase?**  
   ashita na n da kedo tte, nan desu ka? (明日なんだけどって、何ですか。)
   - A. He's asking if Yuki is free tomorrow.
   - B. He's saying tomorrow's plan is still on.
   - C. He's bringing up tomorrow before the bad news.

5. **Kenta said 'ashita no yoru wa daijoubu. uchi ni konai?' (tomorrow evening's fine, want to come over?). You call him back. Will you go to his place tomorrow? What could you say?**  
   ashita, kenta-san no uchi ni ikimasu ka? (明日、健太さんのうちに行きますか。)

### New words

- **shigoto** (仕事): work, a job
- **yoru** (夜): evening, night
- **gohan** (ご飯): a meal, dinner (also cooked rice)
- **tsukuru** (作る): to make, to cook (verb)
- **denwa** (電話): phone, phone call (denwa suru = to call; denwa dekiru? = can you call?)
- **ame** (雨): rain
