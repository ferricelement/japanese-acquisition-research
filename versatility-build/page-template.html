Japanese Versatility List

The words, endings and patterns that work in almost any conversation. Casual form first, with the polite (desu/masu) form beside it. Tap a row for its meaning and example sentences.

Frequency ranks (#) come from jiten.moe's word-frequency list built from 3,030 Japanese TV dramas (CC BY-SA 4.0). Rank 1 is the most frequent word. * means the counting tool splits the form, so its rank is approximate. Romaji is spelled the way kotoba stores it: no macrons, long vowels written out (sou, daijoubu).
